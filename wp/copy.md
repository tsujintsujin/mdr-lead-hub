## What we check
Heading: What the checkup looks at
1. Speed on a phone. That's where most people go looking for a chiropractor or a PT. When a page drags past a few seconds, plenty of them give up before your phone number even loads.
2. What Google can read. Titles, descriptions, headings, and the small details that tell Google you're a local practice — and where, exactly, you are.
3. How easy booking is. Can someone call or book from the first screen? Or do they have to dig for it?

## Hero

Button: Check my nonprofit

## How it works
Heading: How the check works
1. You tell us about your organization.
2. We find you in the IRS nonprofit records and hold your website up against Google's rules.
3. You see where you stand straight away. Then an Ad Grants specialist follows up with what to do next.

## The rules
Heading: Who Google says yes to
- US charities with 501(c)(3) status
- A working website on https, with real content about your mission
- Not a government agency, a hospital, a school or a university (those go through other programs)
After: Honestly, getting approved is the easy bit. Keeping the grant is harder — Google wants certain click-through numbers and account rules met every month, and that's the part we take off your plate.

## Who we are
Body: Marketing Done Right runs Ad Grants accounts for nonprofits in Ohio and further afield. We set them up, keep them on the right side of Google's rules, and point the ads at what you actually need more of: donors, volunteers, people in your programs.
