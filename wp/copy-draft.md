## What we check
Heading: What the checkup looks at
1. Speed on a phone. Most people find a clinic on their phone. If the page takes more than a few seconds, a lot of them leave before they see your number.
2. What Google can read. Page titles, descriptions, headings, and the details that tell Google you're a local practice and where you are.
3. Whether booking is easy. Can someone call or book from the first screen, without hunting?

Headline: $10,000 a month in Google ads. Free, if you qualify.

Button: Check my nonprofit
Small print: Free. We look up your IRS status for you.

## How it works
Heading: How the check works
1. You tell us about your organization.
2. We look you up in IRS nonprofit records and check your website against Google's rules.
3. You see where you stand right away, and an Ad Grants specialist follows up with what to do next.

## The rules (plain)
Heading: Who Google says yes to
- Registered 501(c)(3) charities in the US
- A working website with real content about your mission, on https
- Not a government agency, hospital, school or university (those have their own programs)
Then: Getting approved is the easy part. Keeping the grant means hitting Google's click-through and account rules every month. That's the part we manage.

## Who we are
Body: Marketing Done Right manages Ad Grants accounts for nonprofits across Ohio and beyond. We set them up, keep them compliant, and point the ads at the things you actually need: donors, volunteers, people for your programs.
