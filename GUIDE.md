# MDR Lead Hub: how it works

A plain guide to starting the demo, what each part does, and what happens to a
lead from the moment someone fills in a form. For every click of the demo video
see [LOOM-SCRIPT.md](LOOM-SCRIPT.md); for how it was built see
[BUILD-LOG.md](BUILD-LOG.md).

## 2. Starting it

| **Docker Desktop** ("Engine running") | Open Docker Desktop | Both WordPress sites (and their databases) |
| **Ollama** with `qwen2.5:7b` | `ollama list` in a terminal | The AI part of scoring |

### Then

Double-click **`start.cmd`** in this folder. It:

1. starts the two WordPress sites in Docker (stops here with a message if
   Docker isn't answering);
2. opens **n8n** in its own window (if port 5678 isn't already in use);
3. opens the **CRM** in a minimised window (if port 3090 isn't in use);
4. opens the **Cloudflare Tunnel** in a minimised window (if it isn't running).

Leave those windows open. Closing one stops that part.

`C:\Users\justi\Scripts\start-docker.ps1` (double-click it), not from the
Start menu inside a Claude session.

## 3. The parts, one by one

**Site A, "Practice Growth Check"** (WordPress, custom block theme in
`wp/themes/practice/`). A free website checkup for clinics. A 3-step form; the
next screen shows real results for the visitor's own website.

**Site B, "Grant Ready"** (WordPress + Elementor, pages generated by
`wp/site-b/build.php`). A free check for nonprofits: could they get Google Ad
Grants ($10,000 a month in free search ads)?

**The form plugin, `mdr-intake`** (`wp/plugins/mdr-intake/`). Used by both
sites. It checks the answers, saves the entry in WordPress, signs it with a
secret key and sends it to n8n. If n8n is down it keeps the entry and retries
every 5 minutes, so nothing is lost.

**n8n** (`n8n/`). All the logic lives here, in 9 workflows. They are built
from code: change `n8n/build.mjs` or `n8n/lib/*`, run `node n8n/test.js`, then
`node n8n/build.mjs`. Edits made by hand in the n8n editor get overwritten.

| Workflow | What it does |
|---|---|
| MDR · Lead intake | The main one. Receives a form, checks, scores, routes and posts it everywhere |
| MDR · Roster | Reads the Sheet's Team tab: who covers which kind of business |
| MDR · Sheet helper | Writes and updates rows in the Sheet's Leads tab |
| MDR · Sync a change everywhere | When a lead changes in one place, copies it to the others |
| MDR · Slack buttons | Handles the buttons on Slack lead cards |
| MDR · Asana updates | Notices when an Asana task is ticked |
| MDR · Follow-up watch | Flags leads nobody has touched for 24 hours |
| MDR · Daily digest | The 8:00 New York time summary in `#mdr-daily` |
| MDR · Error alerts | Posts to `#mdr-ops` if any workflow fails |

**Ollama, `qwen2.5:7b`**. A free AI model on this PC. It reads each lead and
writes a summary, its reasons and an opening line for the salesperson. No paid
AI service is used.

**Lighthouse** (`tools/lh.mjs`). Google's free speed test, run on the visitor's
website as a phone would see it.

**ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer**. Free IRS records, used to check that a
nonprofit is real and see its size.

**The CRM** (`crm/`, Next.js + SQLite). Not WordPress. The sales team's screen:
every lead, its stage, the AI's reasoning, a way to correct the AI, and a way
to hand a lead to someone else. Pages: Leads, Pipeline, Team, Reports.

**Google Sheet**. The **Team** tab decides who gets which kind of lead (edit it
and routing changes, no code). The **Leads** tab is a running log.

**Slack** (workspace MDR Demo). One channel per kind of lead:
`#leads-healthcare`, `#leads-home-services`, `#leads-nonprofit`,
`#leads-general`, plus `#mdr-ops` (alerts) and `#mdr-daily` (summary). Each
lead card has buttons: **Mark contacted**, **Booked a call**, **Not a fit**.

**Asana** (project MDR Lead Follow-ups). A follow-up task for every lead that
needs a person. When a lead is marked **Won**, a whole onboarding project is
created.

**Brevo email**. Was used for the email to the prospect and to the
salesperson. **Currently switched off** (PR #5). Nothing is emailed.

**Cloudflare Tunnel** (`tunnel/`, `mdr-hooks.thejobstash.com`). Slack and Asana
live on the internet and need a way to tell n8n "someone clicked a button".
The tunnel opens only those two webhook paths to this PC; everything else is
blocked. Without it, the sites, scoring and CRM still work, but Slack buttons
and Asana ticks won't sync back.

## 4. What happens to one lead

1. **Visitor submits** the 3-step form on Site A or B.
2. **Plugin** checks it, saves it in WordPress, sends it to n8n signed.
3. **n8n checks the visitor's world:** opens their website, runs Lighthouse,
   and for nonprofits looks them up in IRS records. The visitor's results page
   fills in with these real numbers while the rest carries on.
4. **Rules score it, up to 70 points:** spam signs, organisations Google won't
   fund (hospitals, schools, government agencies), budget, and whether they're in MDR's area
   (north-east Ohio scores highest, then the rest of Ohio).
5. **The AI adds up to 30 points** for how serious the enquiry sounds, and
   writes the summary, reasons and opening line. Past corrections from the CRM
   are fed into its instructions, so it learns the team's judgement.
6. **Verdict from the total:**

   | Score | Verdict |
   |---|---|
   | 70 and up | **Hot** |
   | 45 to 69 | **Warm** |
   | 30 to 44 | **Needs a conversation** |
   | under 30 | **Not a fit** |
   | spam detected | **Spam** (goes to nobody) |

7. **Routing:** n8n reads the Team tab to see who covers that kind of business,
   asks the CRM who of them has the fewest open leads, and gives it to them.
8. **Fan-out:** saved in the CRM, posted in the right Slack channel with
   buttons, an Asana task (if a person needs to act), a row in the Sheet. The
   visitor's results page shows the salesperson's name.
9. **Staying in sync:** a Slack button, an Asana tick or a stage change in the
   CRM is copied to all the other places.
10. **Nothing falls through:** untouched for 24 hours → Slack alert to the
    manager (Marcus Hale). Every morning at 8:00 New York time → summary in
    `#mdr-daily`.

The people (Ana Reyes, Ben Kowalski, Carla Jimenez, Dana Price, Marcus Hale)
are made up.

## 5. Trying it yourself

1. Open the CRM (http://localhost:3090) and note how many leads there are.
2. Open Site A (http://localhost:8081), fill in the form with a real clinic's
   website, submit.
3. Watch the results page fill in (Lighthouse takes up to a minute).
4. Refresh the CRM: the new lead is there with its score, verdict and rep.
5. Open Slack, find the card in that rep's channel, click **Mark contacted**.
6. Back in the CRM the stage changes to Contacted, and the Asana task and
   Sheet row follow.
7. In the CRM, open the lead and set it to **Won**: an onboarding project
   appears in Asana.

Shortcuts (open in a browser tab):

| Address | What it does |
|---|---|
| http://localhost:5678/webhook/mdr-followup-now?hours=0.05 | Runs the "nobody has called" check now, treating 3 minutes as overdue |
| http://localhost:5678/webhook/mdr-digest-now | Posts the daily summary now |

Commands (in a terminal in this folder):

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `node tools/reset-demo.mjs` | Wipes the demo: CRM, Sheet Leads tab, Asana tasks and projects, WordPress entries, Slack lead channels |
| `node tools/seed-demo.mjs` | Sends five sample leads through the real forms (about 5 minutes) |
| `node n8n/test.js` | Checks scoring, routing and messages without sending anything |
| `node n8n/build.mjs` | Rebuilds and uploads every n8n workflow |

## 6. When something doesn't work

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `start.cmd` says "Docker isn't answering" | Docker Desktop is closed or still starting | Start it with `start-docker.ps1`, wait for "Engine running", run `start.cmd` again |
| Docker Desktop says it "is unable to start" / WSL says virtualization isn't enabled | CPU virtualization is turned off in the PC's BIOS/UEFI (seen 2026-11-04: "Virtualization Enabled In Firmware: No") | Restart into BIOS (usually F2 or Del at boot), turn on **Intel VT-x / AMD SVM**, save, boot, start Docker again |
| Sites at 8081/8082 don't load | Docker isn't running | As above |
| Form submits but nothing reaches the CRM | n8n window closed | Run `start.cmd`; the plugin retries every 5 minutes, so the lead will still arrive |
| Leads arrive with no AI summary | Ollama isn't running | Start Ollama, check `ollama list` shows `qwen2.5:7b` |
| Slack buttons / Asana ticks don't sync | Tunnel window closed | Run `start.cmd` (or `start-tunnel.cmd`) |
| Slack card is slow to change | Known: takes 10–20 s, sometimes needs a reload | The change is already saved; reload Slack |
| Site B says "Loads without https" for a site that has https | Known bug with sites that redirect to `www.` | Not fixed yet; ignore for that lead |
| No emails arrive | Email is switched off on purpose | Nothing to fix |
